## What is DishRight?

DishRight is a cooking platform focused on making recipes easier to discover, adapt, and cook.

Recipes are usually written for fixed portions, while real cooking isn't. DishRight explores a system where recipes can adapt to the user's requirements instead of requiring manual calculations.

## The Problem

Cooking often involves:

- Adjusting ingredient quantities for different numbers of people
- Estimating proportions when ingredients are limited
- Finding dishes based on available time or ingredients
- Deciding what to cook
- Finding and sharing recipes

Manual adjustments can result in incorrect proportions, inconsistent taste, and unnecessary food waste.
